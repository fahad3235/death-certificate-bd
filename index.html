How to register a death in Bangladesh

Death registration in Bangladesh runs through BDRIS — the Birth and Death Registration Information System — under the Births and Deaths Registration Act, 2004. Register with the registrar of the Union Parishad, Pourashava, City Corporation or Cantonment Board covering the area where the death occurred. For a death abroad, register at the nearest Bangladesh mission.

Who can apply

The application is generally filed by the deceased's father, mother, spouse, son or daughter.

Steps

  1. Collect the documents listed in the Documents tab.
  2. Apply online at bdris.gov.bd/dr/application — or collect the form at the registrar's office.
  3. Fill in the deceased's details, the date and place of death, and the cause of death as written by the doctor or hospital.
  4. Submit the application and note the application / tracking number.
  5. Visit the registrar's office with the original documents for verification, at the counter or an appointment slot booked through the portal.
  6. Pay the fee, if any (see the Fee Calculator tab).
  7. Collect the printed death certificate from the office once it is approved.

Why you need it

A death certificate is needed sooner than most families expect: for the warish (heirship) certificate, bank accounts and savings certificates, pension and gratuity, insurance claims, land mutation, and closing a mobile number or a trade licence.

Register within 45 days of the death: it is free, and it is the smoothest path. After 45 days a small fee applies, and after 5 years you must also give a written explanation for the delay.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I apply for a death certificate online?

    Yes — apply at bdris.gov.bd/dr/application. Most offices still ask you to bring the original documents in person for verification before the certificate is issued.

    How long do I have to register?

    Registration within 45 days of the death is free and the smoothest path. From 45 days to 5 years a small fee applies; after 5 years you must also submit a written explanation for the delay.

    What if the death happened abroad?

    Deaths abroad are registered at the nearest Bangladesh High Commission, Embassy or Consulate, which acts as the registrar.

    What if the deceased had no birth certificate?

    The registrar can still process the case with the other documents — NID or passport, the doctor's or hospital's death certificate, and the informant's own ID. Ask your local office which papers it will accept.

    Can I get a correction on a certificate?

    Yes — apply to the same registrar's office with the correct documents and the original certificate. Corrections are verified by the office before a revised certificate is issued.
